Your situation

Set the frame for this score.

Goal changes the order of actions. It does not hide a risk. Practice type and state decide whether an orientation range can appear.

1

Do you have three years of complete financial statements and tax returns?

2

Can you clearly separate owner-specific and one-time costs from practice earnings?

3

Do you track monthly collections, overhead, and profit against a plan?

4

Do you understand your payer mix and any concentration or reimbursement risks?

5

Is your lease assignable, with enough term and options for a new owner?

6

Could the practice maintain patient care and revenue if you stepped away?

7

Are team roles, compensation, and key employment arrangements documented?

8

Are your core systems and reports consistent and easy for a buyer to review?

9

Have you compared private-sale, DSO affiliation, and recap options?

10

Do you have a written transition plan and a target timeline?

Local market snapshot (coming soon)

These five free public sources are the ones a later version could use. This mockup does not pull them in, and it shows no market numbers.

  1. NPPES NPI Registry (CMS). Nearby dentists and specialists.
  2. Census American Community Survey. Local population, age and income.
  3. Census County Business Patterns. Number and size of dental offices.
  4. BLS Occupational Wages (OEWS). Local pay for hygienists, assistants and dentists.
  5. BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW). Local dental job and wage trends.
