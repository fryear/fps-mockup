The Frye Transition Process

A proven 5-step approach to selling your dental practice with confidence.

1

Confidential Consultation

We discuss your goals, timeline, and what makes your practice unique.

2

Practice Valuation

A thorough 3-method analysis to determine fair market value.

3

Prospectus & Marketing

A professional offering memorandum marketed to our qualified buyer network.

4

Buyer Matching

We identify the right buyer and negotiate terms that protect your interests.

5

Close & Transition

Coordinated closing with attorneys, CPAs, and lenders. Smooth handoff.

Our Team

Meet the Team

Karl started Frye Practice Sales after more than a decade in commercial banking, specializing in healthcare M&A and helping doctors obtain the credit to grow and sell their practices. He saw the gap: dental owners needed a broker who understood both the clinical and financial sides of a transition.

Karl leads every engagement as principal broker, supported by a growing FPS team.

Malachi Frye, Director of Growth and Expansion, is joining FPS to grow the team and lead our expansion to Southern California.
