Local market snapshot (coming soon)

These five free public sources are the ones a later version could use. This mockup does not pull them in, and it shows no market numbers.

  1. NPPES NPI Registry (CMS). Nearby dentists and specialists.
  2. Census American Community Survey. Local population, age and income.
  3. Census County Business Patterns. Number and size of dental offices.
  4. BLS Occupational Wages (OEWS). Local pay for hygienists, assistants and dentists.
  5. BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW). Local dental job and wage trends.
